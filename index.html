About Us

Where Every Child is a Treasure

At Efficient Treasure Academy, we believe every child carries unique potential waiting to be discovered and refined. Located in the heart of Gbuduwyi, Kabusa in the Federal Capital Territory, Abuja, we are committed to delivering quality, efficient, and values-driven education.

Our approach combines strong academic foundations with character development, ensuring students not only excel in their studies but also grow into responsible, confident, and purposeful individuals.

We create a safe, nurturing, and stimulating learning environment where curiosity is encouraged, excellence is celebrated, and every learner is supported to reach their full potential.
